# SignalGate — Growth & Marketing Plan (v2, comprehensive edition)
### Eleven specialist lenses · staggered 10 → 50 → 100 → 1000 acquisition ladder · proven + experimental methods

Governing rule inherited from the product audit — non-negotiable and threaded
through every tactic below: **sell discipline and auditability, never coverage
or returns. No performance claims until a real record exists. Demo-only, and
say so.** Every tactic is tagged **[PROVEN]** (works reliably in this or
adjacent categories) or **[EXPERIMENTAL]** (high-upside bet; run small, kill
fast).

# PART I — THE STRATEGIC CORE

## 1. The one insight everything hangs on: trust inversion

The trading-signals category is the most distrusted corner of retail finance.
Every competitor claims wins — photoshopped screenshots, fake equity curves,
rented lifestyles. The audience's default emotional state is **burned
skepticism**. Therefore the only unclaimed position in the category is
**verifiable honesty**, and SignalGate's ledger — which records losses as
losses — is the only asset in the category that can occupy it.

**Positioning line:** *The only trading tool that shows you its losses.*

**Mechanism (costly signalling):** publishing losses is expensive to fake.
Scammers cannot copy it without destroying their own funnel. A signal that is
costly to fake is the only signal a burned audience believes. This is a moat
made of incentives, not code.

**Category villain:** the fake-screenshot signal seller. Never name people;
name the *pattern*, relentlessly.

## 2. The two-sided wedge (who, exactly)

- **Primary ICP — signal providers** (the Nick archetype): Telegram/Discord
  providers, 50–5,000 followers, whose reputation dies every time a follower
  executes badly and blames them. Their product: **an independent, timestamped
  forward-test record of their own signals** — the proof asset nobody in the
  category has. **Each provider is also a distribution channel**: onboarding
  one puts cards and ledgers in front of his entire follower base. B2B2C.
- **Secondary ICP — disciplined signal-followers**: already pay for signals,
  lose on *execution* (late entries, no stop, revenge trades). Their product:
  rulebook execution + their own audit record.
- **Anti-ICP (turn away, loudly):** get-rich-quick hunters, "what's the win
  rate" leads, anyone asking us to trade their money. Turning them away is
  itself a trust signal — and legally necessary.

# PART II — THE ELEVEN SPECIALIST LENSES

Each lens = a named discipline, its verdict on this product, and its
non-obvious contribution. This is the panel v1 compressed; here it is in full.

### Lens 1 — Direct-response strategist (Ogilvy/Halbert school)
**Verdict:** the category's DR playbook (urgency, greed, proof) is poisoned —
greed appeals attract the anti-ICP and legal risk. Invert it: the "problem"
is *distrust*, the "proof" is the ledger, the CTA is low-commitment.
**Contribution:** lead with the *demonstration*, not the claim. The glass-box
walkthrough is a classic "prove it in front of them" DR device (the Schlitz
brewery tour move). Long-form landing copy works here because burned buyers
research obsessively — give them everything to verify. One CTA per asset:
"Watch it work — 15 minutes, your phone, nothing at risk."

### Lens 2 — Behavioural economist (Kahneman/Cialdini/Thaler)
**Verdict:** the product is unusually rich in honest behavioural levers; the
danger is using the category's *dishonest* ones out of habit.
**Contribution — the lever map (all [PROVEN] as mechanisms):**
- **Loss aversion:** sell the cost of sloppy execution, not the joy of winning.
  "The gap between the signal and your fill is where your money dies."
- **Costly signalling:** "We publish our losing trades" as the hero line.
- **Social proof (real only):** the live forward-test ledger IS the proof; no
  testimonials until real, consented ones exist.
- **Commitment & consistency:** the YES tap is a commitment device; a 14-day
  Demo Desk builds a daily habit; streaks in the weekly digest.
- **Endowment + IKEA effect:** the tester co-produces *their* record with every
  tap; it's theirs to export and keep. People defend what they built.
- **Honest scarcity:** pilot seats genuinely capped by concierge capacity —
  "Seat 4 of 10." Never a fake counter; the audience checks.
- **Identity:** "operators, not gamblers"; receipts culture. People adopt tools
  that signal who they are.
- **Peak–end rule:** engineer Watch It Work so the peak is *their tap* causing
  a live demo execution + ledger row, and the end is receiving their first
  exported record.
- **Defaults as marketing:** hard stop always in; missed card expires to *no
  trade*. Sell the constraints — they are the product.
- **Hyperbolic discounting, countered:** "your record gets more valuable every
  week it exists" — time becomes an asset, the opposite of urgency tricks.

### Lens 3 — Category designer (Ries & Trout / *Play Bigger* school)
**Verdict:** don't compete in "signal services" — you lose by association.
Create the adjacent category: **execution discipline & verification tooling**.
**Contribution:** name the category ("verified execution"), name the problem
("execution leak"), name the enemy pattern ("screenshot proof"). Write the
one-page category manifesto: *Why trading needs receipts.* Category creators
take ~2 years to win but own the language; every "show me your ledger" utterance
is category marketing. Lightning-strike moment: the first Verification Pack.

### Lens 4 — Viral-loop engineer (PLG school)
**Verdict:** classic invite-a-friend loops are weak here (trading is private,
often embarrassing). The strong loops are **artifact-led** and **provider-led**.
**Contribution — three loops, ranked:**
1. **Provider loop [PROVEN, the engine]:** provider joins → his followers see
   cards → some become testers → testers' records circulate → next provider
   asks in. Viral coefficient lives on the *provider* side: one provider = a
   whole audience. Optimize time-to-first-provider-value (<1 week).
2. **Artifact loop [PROVEN mechanism, EXPERIMENTAL format]:** the
   auto-generated weekly ledger card (wins AND losses, clean design, watermark,
   short verification link). It spreads because it's counter-positional.
   Instrument every share.
3. **Challenge loop [EXPERIMENTAL]:** "Verify Your Provider" — followers tag
   providers to demand it; accepting providers enter the funnel; declining
   providers send their followers a message we never say aloud. Never
   name-and-shame; the offer does the work.

### Lens 5 — Community-led growth operator (Telegram/Discord/Reddit native)
**Verdict:** this audience lives in closed rooms (Telegram groups, prop-firm
Discords) — invisible to ads, allergic to pitches, moved by *journals and
receipts*.
**Contribution:** the **public forward-test journal** is the single best
community asset — ForexFactory and TradingView both have journal cultures
where daily honest updates build cult followings [PROVEN]. Post the journal,
answer questions, never pitch; let the profile link do the work. In Reddit
(r/Forex, r/Daytrading), contribute scam-anatomy education and AMA only when
the Verification Pack ships. Build our own Telegram channel as the home room:
cards (anonymised), weekly drops, "loss weeks" posted with the same energy as
win weeks.

### Lens 6 — Content & SEO strategist
**Verdict:** the audience *googles their distrust*. Nobody in the category
answers honestly — free compounding real estate.
**Contribution:** two content tracks. **Trust track [PROVEN SEO]:** "how to
verify a signal provider," "are trading signals a scam," "why signal followers
lose money," "what is forward testing" — each ends at the honest alternative.
**Journal track:** the weekly forward-test update repurposed 4 ways (long-form
post → YouTube 5-min → X thread → ledger card image). One artifact, four
channels, once a week — sustainable for a solo founder. YouTube long-form
scam teardowns are the authority play; Shorts/TikTok clips of the glass-box
demo are the reach play ("watch a signal become a demo trade with a hard
stop, live" / "this is our losing week — here's why we publish it").

### Lens 7 — PR & earned-media operator
**Verdict:** "startup launches tool" is not a story. **"A trading tool
published its losses every week for 90 days"** is a story. So is "the
challenge that sorts honest signal providers from fakes."
**Contribution:** build the **newsjack kit** now [EXPERIMENTAL, high upside]:
when the next big signal-scam story breaks (one always does), publish the
same-day teardown + the category manifesto — journalists covering the scam
need the "what should exist instead" quote. Pitch trading-skeptic and
scam-buster YouTubers (perfect-fit audiences that ads can't buy) with the
Verification Pack as the hook. Personal-story angle for podcasts: *"I built
receipts for the most scammed corner of the internet."*

### Lens 8 — Performance-marketing specialist (paid)
**Verdict:** paid is **premature before 100 users** and hostile terrain:
finance ads face platform restrictions, compliance review, and CPCs bid up by
the scammers themselves. And a trust-inversion brand buying hype ads is
self-contradicting.
**Contribution:** hold paid until (a) the Verification Pack exists, (b) hosted
infra can absorb signups, (c) promotions counsel has cleared the creative.
Then the only creative worth testing is the honest one: **advertise the losing
week** [EXPERIMENTAL] — "We lost 2.1R last week. Here's the full record." —
against retargeting/lookalikes of journal readers. Budget cap ~£500/mo test;
kill if CAC > pilot-seat value. Until then, £0 on ads.

### Lens 9 — Partnerships & BD
**Verdict:** the highest-leverage doors are the ones already full of the ICP.
**Contribution:** ranked pipeline — (1) **more signal providers** (each is
customer + channel; the Verify Your Provider challenge is the outreach
engine); (2) **prop-firm communities [EXPERIMENTAL]** — prop firms profit when
traders follow rules; a discipline tool is aligned; offer a cohort pilot;
(3) **trading educators/journal YouTubers** — co-created "verified forward
test" series; (4) **broker demo-account referrals [EXPERIMENTAL, later]** —
brokers want engaged demo users; strict compliance review first. Do NOT
partner with signal-selling groups for shoutouts — adjacency taint.

### Lens 10 — Lifecycle & retention (CRM)
**Verdict:** acquisition is wasted if testers go quiet after week one;
retention here = the card-response habit + the growing record.
**Contribution:** the **weekly ledger digest** is the retention spine [PROVEN]:
your cards, your decisions, your R, your streak — plus one community stat.
Lifecycle sequence: Day 0 welcome (what happens next, when cards come) → first
card follow-up ("your first row exists — this is your record now") → week-2
"here's what your report will look like at day 14" → day-14 **Discipline
Report** delivery (the endowment moment) → invite to Pilot waitlist. Dormancy
save: "your record paused — want back in?" Instrument card-sent → decision
rate as the core health metric; it also tests the 5-minute expiry with data.

### Lens 11 — Compliance-aware fintech marketer (the veto seat)
**Verdict:** UK financial-promotions territory. One fake proof artifact or
earnings claim doesn't just risk regulatory pain — it vaporises the entire
positioning. This lens holds a veto over every asset.
**Contribution:** the standing rules — no performance/profit/earnings claims,
ever, until the real record exists, then publish it whole (losses, drawdown,
identifiers, date range). Risk line on every asset: *trading carries risk of
loss; most retail traders lose money; demo-only v1; execution tooling, not
advice.* No fake urgency/testimonials/badges. **One hour of
financial-promotions counsel before**: any paid tier, any "results" framing,
any live-trading language. Every published number must trace to a ledger row.

# PART III — THE STAGGERED ACQUISITION LADDER

Different stages are different *problems*. Each stage has a goal, a primary
motion, plays (tagged), exit criteria, and — critically — **product gates**
from the audit/roadmap, because marketing past your infrastructure is how you
burn trust you can't rebuild.

## Stage 1 — First 10 users (weeks 0–4): the founder-led proof cohort
**The problem type:** a *founder* problem. Zero leverage, all hustle.
Do things that don't scale (Paul Graham) — recruit by hand, onboard 1:1.

- **Goal:** 10 *real, active* testers (respond to ≥60% of cards weekly) +
  provider #1 (Nick) live. Not signups — *active* testers.
- **Primary motion:** personal invitation through Nick. His followers are the
  warmest possible pool: they already follow signals; their pain is execution.
- **Plays:**
  - **Nick announces to his own group [PROVEN]:** "I'm forward-testing my own
    signals through an independent tool for 8 weeks — 10 seats to test it with
    me, free, demo accounts, you keep your record." Provider-endorsed scarcity,
    fully honest.
  - **Watch It Work, 1:1 [PROVEN]:** every prospect gets the 15-minute
    phone-in-hand session (peak–end engineered). Founder-led onboarding at this
    stage doubles as user research.
  - **The named cohort [PROVEN]:** "Forward Test Cohort 1" — a start date, an
    end date, a private Telegram room, a weekly review call. Cohorts create
    commitment, identity, and a shared finish line.
  - **Concierge everything [PROVEN]:** Ben personally fixes every stumble
    within hours. At n=10, support IS marketing.
- **Experimental bets:** film every Watch It Work session (with consent) —
  raw clips become Stage 2–3 content [EXPERIMENTAL]; "burned by signals"
  intake question — capture each tester's worst execution story as future
  (consented) narrative material [EXPERIMENTAL].
- **Exit criteria:** 10 active testers · card→decision rate >60% · forward
  test running 2+ consecutive weeks · 3+ testers spontaneously described the
  product accurately to someone else (message-market fit signal).
- **Product gates:** the P0 fixes live; simulator demo path stable; ledger
  honest (stop-outs as losses — verified).
- **Do NOT yet:** public channels, press, paid, provider #2. Focus is oxygen.

## Stage 2 — 10 → 50 (weeks 4–10): the community beachhead
**The problem type:** a *community* problem. Leave the warm pool; earn
strangers' trust with receipts, not reach.

- **Goal:** 50 active testers, 2–3 providers live, the journal habit public.
- **Primary motion:** **build in public with artifacts.** The forward test
  goes public and becomes the content engine — nothing publishes without it.
- **Plays:**
  - **Public forward-test journal [PROVEN]:** weekly, same format, everywhere
    at once (long-form + YouTube 5-min + X thread + ledger card): what won,
    what lost, R, and one lesson. Losses posted with the same energy as wins.
  - **ForexFactory / TradingView journal threads [PROVEN]:** these forums'
    native format; daily/weekly honest updates build followings. Never pitch
    in-thread.
  - **Verify Your Provider challenge — wave 1 [EXPERIMENTAL]:** personally
    offer 10 hand-picked small providers a free 30-day independent forward
    test. Accepters become providers #2–3 (each bringing followers). Public
    but not aggressive; no naming-and-shaming.
  - **Referral = a seat [PROVEN]:** each Cohort-1 tester may invite one person
    to Cohort 2. A capped, real invitation — status, not commission.
  - **Scam-anatomy series begins [PROVEN SEO + authority]:** 2/month,
    evergreen, each ending at the honest alternative.
  - **Glass-box livestream #1 [EXPERIMENTAL]:** signal → card → tap → demo
    execution → ledger row, live, no cuts. Recording becomes the permanent
    "how it works" asset.
- **Experimental bets:** Telegram sticker pack ("APPROVED ✅ / EXPIRED ⏱ /
  SHOW ME YOUR LEDGER") seeded in trading groups — culture artifacts travel
  where ads can't [EXPERIMENTAL]; "signal autopsy" micro-tool — paste any
  signal text, get the R-math and risk read (shareable, lead magnet, shows the
  parser's honesty) [EXPERIMENTAL].
- **Exit criteria:** 50 actives · ≥2 providers · journal shipped 6 consecutive
  weeks · first inbound "can I join?" requests arriving weekly · waitlist
  started.
- **Product gates (from the roadmap):** P1 fixes (bot auth, per-tenant
  scoping) before non-Nick providers; `/myrecord` + weekly digest shipped
  (retention before acquisition); admin tooling for 50 users.
- **Do NOT yet:** paid ads, PR push, pricing.

## Stage 3 — 50 → 100 (weeks 10–16): the proof moment
**The problem type:** a *proof* problem. You now have the one asset nobody in
the category has ever shown. Spend it well.

- **Goal:** 100 actives, a waitlist bigger than capacity, the category named.
- **Primary motion:** **the first Verification Pack** — the real 60–90-day
  demo record from the ledger: every trade, every loss, drawdown, date range,
  account identifier, a way to check it. This is the lightning strike.
- **Plays:**
  - **Verification Pack launch [PROVEN mechanics, category-first content]:**
    one URL, permanent, plainly designed. Launch it as a *story*: "A trading
    tool published its losses for 90 days. Here's the record."
  - **Skeptic-creator pitch wave [PROVEN PR]:** scam-buster and
    trading-skeptic YouTubers/podcasts get the Pack as the hook. Their
    audiences are pre-sold on the villain; we're the receipts they demand.
  - **Reddit AMA with the Pack [PROVEN]:** receipts first, promotion never.
  - **Category manifesto ships [Lens 3]:** *Why trading needs receipts* — the
    naming document, referenced in every asset from now on.
  - **Newsjack kit armed [EXPERIMENTAL]:** the next signal-scam headline gets
    a same-day teardown + manifesto quote.
  - **Cohort waitlist machine [PROVEN]:** numbered seats, published cadence
    ("Cohort 3 opens on the 1st, 25 seats"), waitlist nurtured by the weekly
    digest.
- **Experimental bets:** "advertise the losing week" organic test — pin the
  worst week as the channel trailer and measure follow-through
  [EXPERIMENTAL]; provider leaderboard by *record completeness* (never by
  returns) [EXPERIMENTAL, compliance-check first].
- **Exit criteria:** 100 actives · waitlist > next cohort capacity · 1–2
  earned-media placements · inbound provider pipeline ≥5.
- **Product gates:** hosted deployment live (TLS, real auth, license gating
  ON for external EAs) — **hard gate; do not pass 100 on localhost** ·
  financial-promotions counsel completed · written pilot terms.

## Stage 4 — 100 → 1000 (months 4–12): the compounding machine
**The problem type:** an *infrastructure* problem — of systems, content
compounding, and network effects. New rule: **nothing scales that isn't
already working small.**

- **Goal:** 1,000 active users across 15–30 providers; the provider network
  is the moat; revenue exists (pilot seats / provider desks) with counsel-
  approved terms.
- **Primary motion:** **the provider network effect.** At ~10 providers the
  flywheel self-feeds: providers recruit providers (the record is their
  competitive weapon against unverified rivals), followers arrive with them.
  Marketing's job shifts to arming providers: white-labelled weekly record
  cards, a provider directory ranked by verification depth, co-marketing kits.
- **Plays:**
  - **Provider-as-channel program [PROVEN B2B2C]:** every provider gets
    shareable record assets + a referral seat pool for their followers.
    10 providers × 100 followers ≈ the whole target on its own.
  - **SEO/content compounding [PROVEN]:** by now 6+ months of journals,
    teardowns, and the Pack; double down on what ranks; the library sells
    while you sleep.
  - **YouTube as the trust archive [PROVEN]:** weekly forward-test videos +
    teardowns become the binge-funnel; burned buyers research for hours —
    give them hours.
  - **Lifecycle automation [PROVEN]:** the Lens-10 sequence fully automated;
    dormancy saves; cohort graduation → pilot upsell.
  - **First paid tests [EXPERIMENTAL, gated]:** only now — counsel-cleared,
    honest creative ("We lost 2.1R last week — full record here"), capped
    budget, retargeting journal readers first; kill at CAC > seat value.
  - **Prop-firm cohort pilots [EXPERIMENTAL]:** one firm, one cohort,
    measured; scale only on retention parity.
  - **Community summit / live forward-test event [EXPERIMENTAL]:** the
    receipts-culture flagship moment; also a press hook.
- **Exit criteria (i.e., the next plan):** 1,000 actives · ≥15 providers ·
  organic inbound > outbound · revenue covering infrastructure · a repeatable
  cohort machine.
- **Product gates:** the full "before paid users" and "before hosted/public
  launch" lists from the audit (auth rebuild, UUID PKs, atomic claims, TLS,
  Postgres exercised) · support capacity (a part-time human or a very good
  help flow) · **live trading remains a separate, legally-reviewed decision —
  it is NOT a growth lever in this plan.**

# PART IV — OPERATING SYSTEM

## Solo-founder weekly cadence (≤8 hrs/week on marketing)
- **Mon (1h):** ledger export → weekly card + digest.
- **Tue (2h):** the week's journal artifact (write once) → repurpose to 4
  formats.
- **Wed (1h):** community replies (forums, Telegram, comments). Never pitch.
- **Thu (2h):** stage-specific motion (Stage 1: two Watch It Work sessions ·
  Stage 2: challenge outreach ×3 · Stage 3: creator pitches ×3 · Stage 4:
  provider enablement).
- **Fri (1h):** metrics review vs stage exit criteria; kill/scale decisions.
- **Rule:** if a week gets squeezed, the journal ships and everything else
  slips. The journal is the heartbeat; a missed week breaks the costly-
  signalling streak that IS the brand.

## Budget tiers
- **£0 (Stages 1–2):** everything above works unpaid. Time is the spend.
- **£100/mo (Stage 3):** design polish for the Pack + ledger cards, a
  scheduling tool, domain/email.
- **£500/mo (Stage 4):** the capped paid experiments, video editing help,
  lifecycle tooling. Never more until CAC is proven under seat value.

## Kill criteria & pivot signals (pre-committed, so momentum can't argue)
- Card→decision rate <40% after coaching → the 5-minute expiry or card UX is
  wrong; fix product before adding users.
- Watch It Work → active-tester conversion <30% → the pitch or the session is
  wrong; rework before scaling outreach.
- Challenge wave: <2 of 10 providers accept → the provider offer is
  mis-framed; re-interview providers, rewrite the offer.
- Journal at 8 weeks with near-zero inbound → distribution problem, not
  content: shift format/venue, don't add volume.
- Any tactic that only works when it exaggerates → kill it that day,
  whatever the numbers say.

## The do-not-do list (each would import the scam pattern)
Buy followers/engagement · fake scarcity or countdowns · paid shoutouts in
signal-selling groups · profit-porn creative "just for reach" · pay-per-signup
affiliate armies · testimonials before real, consented ones exist · win-rate
leaderboards · any number the ledger can't back · any hint of "make money"
messaging. **The brand is the receipts. One fake anything and the only
unclaimed position in the category is gone for good.**

## Metrics stack (by stage)
- **Always:** card→decision rate · weekly digest opens · journal-artifact
  shares · waitlist net-adds by source.
- **Stage 1:** active-tester count, session→active conversion.
- **Stage 2:** journal follower growth, challenge accept rate, referral-seat
  uptake.
- **Stage 3:** Pack traffic → waitlist conversion, earned-media placements,
  inbound provider requests.
- **Stage 4:** provider count & their follower-activation rate, organic:
  outbound ratio, CAC vs seat value, cohort retention (week-2 and week-8).
