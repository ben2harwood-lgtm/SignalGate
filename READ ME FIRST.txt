IMPORTANT SAFETY / SCOPE
------------------------
- Demo accounts only.
- Explicit subscriber authorisation remains required per transaction.
- Providers use isolated provider/feed credentials and tenant-scoped data.
- Subscribers join through one-time consent invites.
- Hosted provider Telegram sources bind through feed-scoped one-time source tokens.
- Do not use the historical shared /provider invite-code flow.
- Do not share .env files, API keys, customer licences or broker credentials.
- Green CI is not MetaEditor, penetration-test or regulatory acceptance.
