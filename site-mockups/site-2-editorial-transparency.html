How it works

Four steps. No black box.

1
Signal in

Your signal provider sends the call to SignalGate — typed, or as a screenshot the system reads and shows back for confirmation. (Auto-reading your channel is on the roadmap, not in this version.)

2
Structured, not raw

It's parsed into a clean trade card — symbol, direction, entry, stop, take-profits. Raw text never reaches MetaTrader; only validated, structured commands do.

3
You approve

The card arrives in your Telegram with the numbers laid out. One tap: Approve or Ignore. Nothing happens without you.

4
Executed & managed

On approval it opens in MetaTrader with a hard stop, scales out across TP1 / TP2 / TP3, moves the stop to breakeven as targets hit, and logs every action.

One-time-only

Every command is one-time-only — a double tap can't double your trade, and a stale signal can't fire.

Straight answers

The things you're right to ask.

Is this just another Telegram get-rich channel?
No. Raw signals never reach MetaTrader — only structured, validated, approved commands. You see and approve every trade, and every action is written to an audit log you can inspect. There's no track record yet — SignalGate is in forward-testing — so we publish none; independent reconciliation is planned before any live discussion.
Do I have to babysit it?
You approve each trade with one tap — only when you want to. Ignore a card and nothing happens. You're in control, not on call.
Can I lose money?
Yes. Most retail traders lose money, and a tool doesn't change that. v1 is demo-only; live trading carries real risk, and with leverage you can lose more than you deposit.
Is a robot trading for me?
No autonomous trading. SignalGate executes only what you approve, under a fixed rulebook, with a hard stop every time.
What do I need to start?
A Telegram account and MetaTrader 5 on a demo account. Budget about 45 minutes for setup the first time — we walk you through it. For always-on execution your MetaTrader needs to stay running; most testers use a low-cost MT5 host (~$10/month), and we help you set that up in the Pilot.
Which markets can I trade?
Forex pairs (majors, minors and crosses — EURUSD, GBPJPY, USDJPY, and the rest), plus gold/silver and major crypto. Your broker just needs those symbols enabled; the executor matches your broker's naming automatically. Stock indices and other CFDs aren't supported in this version.
What happens while I'm asleep?
Cards wait for you and expire after 5 minutes if untouched — a missed card is simply no trade. Nothing fires without your tap.
