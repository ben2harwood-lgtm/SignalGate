The gap

Following a good signal is easy. Executing it well is the hard part.

The call comes in while you're at work, asleep, or driving. You catch it late and the entry's gone. You size it on a feeling. You move the stop "just this once." You let a loser run. The edge was never just the signal — it's the execution, and that's exactly where people leak money.

LEAK 01

Missed entries

The signal lands when you can't act. By the time you see it, the price has gone.

LEAK 02

Late, emotional fills

You chase the move and get in worse than the call — fear and FOMO doing the sizing.

LEAK 03

Wrong size, moved stops

Position too big, then the stop slides "just this once" to avoid taking the loss.

LEAK 04

No discipline taking profit

No plan for scaling out, so wins round-trip back to flat or worse.

How it works

Four steps. No black box.

Every stage is documented and observable. Nothing is inferred, nothing is hidden — here is the exact procedure a signal follows from your channel to a managed demo trade.

Execution procedure · SG-PROC-01 demo-only · you approve every trade
01

Signal in

A signal posts in your channel. SignalGate reads it the moment it lands — no copy-paste, no manual entry, no waiting on you to be at the screen.

Source: your channel
02

Structured, not raw

It's parsed into a clean trade card — symbol, direction, entry, stop, take-profits. Raw text never reaches MetaTrader; only validated, structured commands do.

Validated → command
03

You approve

The card arrives in your Telegram with the numbers laid out. One tap: Approve or Ignore. Nothing happens without you.

Approve / Ignore
04

Executed & managed

On approval it opens in MetaTrader with a hard stop, scales out across TP1 / TP2 / TP3, moves the stop to breakeven as targets hit, and logs every action.

Hard stop · staged TP · logged
Every command is one-time-only — a double tap can't double your trade, and a stale signal can't fire.

Straight answers

Q1

Is this just another Telegram get-rich channel?

Q2

Do I have to babysit it?

You approve each trade with one tap — only when you want to. Ignore a card and nothing happens. You're in control, not on call.

Q3

Yes. Most retail traders lose money, and a tool doesn't change that. v1 is demo-only; live trading carries real risk, and with leverage you can lose more than you deposit.

Q4

Is a robot trading for me?

No autonomous trading. SignalGate executes only what you approve, under a fixed rulebook, with a hard stop every time.
