SignalGate · competitor teardown

Everyone automates the trade. Nobody proves the record.

The strategy-automation space is crowded — the automation itself is commoditised (some tools are free). Read the last column down the page: every one of them leans on a backtest or a selective stat, and none give you a live, auditable forward record. That empty column is the only defensible place to stand.

The one gap they all share
Strip away the interfaces and they're the same bet: trust my backtest, or trust my leaderboard. Both are exactly what blows up on a 2–6 month cycle when live losses arrive. SignalGate's wedge isn't the automation — it's being the only one whose numbers you can check while they happen. In a category built on unverifiable curves, "audit it yourself" is a position almost no one occupies.
