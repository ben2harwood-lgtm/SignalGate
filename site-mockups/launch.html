The rulebook — fixed, not discretionary

  1. A hard stop on every trade. Nothing is ever left un-stopped. Your loss on any one trade is bounded before it opens.
  2. You approve every trade. One tap creates exactly one command. The same approval can never fire twice.
  3. Take-profits are staged. Partial exits at TP1 / TP2 / TP3, and the stop moves to protect the trade as targets are hit.
  4. Miss a card and it expires. Cards live 5 minutes, then it's simply no trade. On purpose.
  5. Every action is logged. Entry, fill, each stop move, each close — written to an audit ledger, wins and losses alike.
