How it works

Four steps. No black box.

Every stage is visible, and you stay the decision-maker. Here is the whole pipeline, start to finish.

01SIGNAL IN

The signal lands

A signal posts in your channel. SignalGate reads it the moment it lands — no copying, no retyping, no waiting on you to be at the screen.

● source: your channel · read on arrival
02STRUCTURED

Parsed into a clean trade card — not raw text

It's parsed into a structured trade card — symbol, direction, entry, stop, take-profits. Raw text never reaches MetaTrader; only validated, structured commands do. Anything ambiguous is rejected, not guessed.

03YOU APPROVE

You approve — one tap

The card arrives in your Telegram with the numbers laid out. One tap: Approve or Ignore. Nothing happens without you. Ignore it and there is simply no trade.

▸ Approve / Ignore · you're the last word
04EXECUTED

Executed & managed by the rulebook

On approval it opens in MetaTrader with a hard stop, scales out across TP1 / TP2 / TP3, moves the stop to breakeven as targets hit, and logs every action — so there's a record you can check afterwards.
