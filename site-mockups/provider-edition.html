What must be proved before production.

Release gates are evidence requirements, not marketing adjectives.

Cross-tenant isolation

Before multi-provider production, a provider credential must be proved unable to read or act on another tenant.

Retry safety

Network retries must not create a second command or replay lifecycle state.

Fail-closed input

Conflicting symbols, stops, targets or stale signals do not proceed.

Recovery

Unknown broker outcomes must be reconciled, never blindly retried.

Release status

Demo-only hardening is intentional.

SignalGate is not being presented here as a completed live-trading service. Production multi-tenancy, provider UI, operational recovery, independent penetration testing and the final UK regulatory-perimeter review are explicit release gates before any retail real-money launch.
