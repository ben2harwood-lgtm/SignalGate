06

What we won't do (the guardrails are the brand)

  • No fabricated performance, testimonials, or win rates. None are claimed until earned — then they're published in full, drawdown and all.
  • A hard stop on every trade. Loss is bounded before the trade opens; the tool can't run away with an account.
  • A human in the loop, demo-first. You approve trades; no live money until the record proves it out.
  • Losses shown as loud as wins. The Truth Room is read-only and can't be edited after the fact.

Trading carries a high risk of loss and most retail traders lose money. SignalGate is execution tooling and a record of it — not financial advice, not a recommendation, and not a promise of profit. v1 is a demo-only forward test; any results shown are labelled as such and are not indicative of future performance. No performance is claimed because none is assumed — the record builds in public, from trade one.
